# AEGIS – Clinical Risk Intelligence Engine

AEGIS is a nonlinear clinical decision support prototype designed to enhance patient safety through multi-domain risk integration and contextual amplification modeling.

This system integrates established clinical assessment tools into a unified computational risk framework.

---

Access the interactive prototype here:

## 🔬 Clinical Domains Integrated

AEGIS incorporates:

- National Early Warning Score (NEWS2)
- Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS)
- Morse Fall Scale
- Braden Scale
- Richmond Agitation-Sedation Scale (RASS)
- Glycemic Risk Assessment

Rather than presenting isolated scores, AEGIS applies dynamic weighting and nonlinear amplification logic to produce a Global Risk Index.

---

## ⚙️ Core Technical Features

- Multi-domain score normalization (0–100 scaling)
- Context-aware service-based weighting
- Nonlinear hybrid risk amplification
- Automated risk profiling (Standard / Elevated / Critical)
- Clinical recommendation engine
- Domain breakdown visualization
- PDF clinical risk report generation

---

## 🧠 Innovation Approach

AEGIS moves beyond linear summation models by:

- Detecting risk clustering patterns
- Amplifying co-occurring critical thresholds
- Applying contextual sensitivity based on clinical service
- Translating multi-factor clinical input into a unified intelligence output

This architecture reflects a systems-level patient safety modeling approach rather than a simple scoring calculator.

---

## 📄 Intended Use

AEGIS is designed to support structured clinical decision-making processes.

Final clinical judgment remains the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.

## 🏥 Vision

The long-term objective of AEGIS is to evolve into:
